Discover Lagos

Lagos is Nigeria’s commercial capital and West Africa’s busiest port city. Use this page to learn the facts that matter to visitors and prospective chamber members: people, places, events, and the neighborhoods where business happens.

Demographics

  • Population: about 16 million in Lagos State
  • Median age: about 18 years
  • Languages: English, Yoruba, and Nigerian Pidgin
  • Key sectors: trade, finance, technology, ports, and entertainment

Why Lagos

  • Major seaport and aviation hub
  • Growing creative and technology community
  • Coastal recreation beside a dense business district
