Membership Levels

Free for non-profit organizations.

Premium spotlight placement and VIP events.

NP Membership

No annual fee for registered non-profit organizations.

  • Directory listing with your organization name
  • Access to community networking events
  • Chamber newsletter and event calendar

Bronze Membership

Annual fee with core chamber benefits.

  • All NP benefits
  • Access to monthly networking mixers
  • Basic advertising on the chamber website
  • Member training workshops at the standard rate

Silver Membership

Higher annual fee with expanded marketing and event access.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • VIP seating at selected chamber events
  • Enhanced directory advertising and social posts
  • Discounted exhibitor rates at the annual expo
  • Quarterly business training sessions

Gold Membership

Highest annual fee with premium spotlight placement.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Homepage member spotlight rotation
  • Special VIP event privileges and reserved tables
  • Premium advertising and one-on-one guidance
  • Priority introductions to visiting trade partners
